Fair value gap practice

Every set is freshly generated, so the charts are never the same twice.

Quick rules
  • An FVG is three candles where the wicks of candles 1 and 3 don't overlap.
  • Bullish FVG: candle 1 high to candle 3 low. Bearish FVG: candle 1 low to candle 3 high.
  • A wick into the zone that closes back out means the FVG is holding.
  • A candle body closing beyond the far edge flips it into an inverse FVG (IFVG) with the opposite role.
  • Bullish FVG fails → resistance. Bearish FVG fails → support.
