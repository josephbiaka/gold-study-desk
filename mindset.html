Morning mindset

Read this before the open, every trading day. From Mark Douglas, Trading in the Zone.

The five fundamental truths

If you truly believe these, losing trades stop feeling like threats, and objectivity, accepting risk and staying in the moment come naturally.

  1. Anything can happen.Some trader, somewhere, can always move price against my analysis.
  2. I don't need to know what happens next to make money.My profit comes from my edge playing out over many trades, not from predicting one.
  3. Wins and losses are randomly distributed for any edge.My edge gives me the odds over a series of trades, not the result of the next one.
  4. An edge is only a higher probability of one thing over another.It is never a certainty.
  5. Every moment in the market is unique.A setup that looks like yesterday's doesn't have to end the same way.

Creating a belief in consistency

I am a consistent winner because…

  1. I objectively identify my edges.Clear, rule-based setups, not feelings or hopes.
  2. I predefine the risk of every trade.I know where I'm wrong and what I'll lose before I enter.
  3. I completely accept the risk, or I am willing to let go of the trade.If I can't accept the loss, I don't take the trade.
  4. I act on my edges without reservation or hesitation.When my setup appears, I take it.
  5. I pay myself as the market makes money available to me.I follow my plan for taking profits.
  6. I continually monitor my susceptibility to making errors.I watch for the mistakes that creep in after wins, losses or strong emotions.
  7. I understand the absolute necessity of these principles, so I never violate them.They are non-negotiable.

Douglas says you make this a working belief by repetition: take a series of 20 trades on one edge, exactly as defined, with no exceptions.
